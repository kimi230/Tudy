01   Video Structure Overview

04   Grammar Patterns

Key grammar structures used in the video. Each pattern includes real examples.

Past Habitual with 'would' (contracted 'd)
The contracted form 'I'd' (I would) is used to describe repeated past actions or habits. This is common in storytelling to describe what someone used to do regularly.
'I'd' (I would의 축약형)는 과거에 반복적으로 했던 행동이나 습관을 설명할 때 사용됩니다. 이야기를 할 때 과거의 일상적 행동을 묘사하는 데 흔히 쓰입니다.
Yet I'd sit on these silent retreats,
I'd take naps every chance I got because it was really hard work.
It turns out that...
This expression is used to introduce a surprising or previously unknown fact. It signals that the speaker is about to reveal something that may change the listener's understanding of a situation.
'It turns out that...'은 놀랍거나 이전에 알려지지 않은 사실을 소개할 때 사용됩니다. 상황에 대한 청자의 이해를 바꿀 수 있는 정보를 드러낼 때 쓰이는 신호 표현입니다.
It turns out that we're fighting one of the most evolutionarily conserved learning processes currently known in science,
What if + past tense (Hypothetical suggestion)
The 'What if + past tense' structure is used to propose a hypothetical scenario or suggestion. It softens the suggestion and makes it more tentative and inviting.
'What if + 과거 시제' 구조는 가상의 시나리오나 제안을 할 때 사용됩니다. 제안을 부드럽게 만들고 더 잠정적이고 초대하는 느낌을 줍니다.
What if instead of fighting our brains... we instead tapped into this natural reward-based learning process,
What if instead, we just got really curious about what was happening in our momentary experience?
Rhetorical questions for engagement
Rhetorical questions are asked not to get an answer but to make the audience think. They are a common public speaking technique to engage listeners and transition between ideas.
수사적 질문은 답변을 얻기 위해서가 아니라 청중이 생각하게 만들기 위해 사용됩니다. 청취자의 관심을 끌고 아이디어 간 전환을 하기 위한 일반적인 발표 기법입니다.
So why is it so hard to pay attention?
So what's going on here?
Simple, right?
What does curiosity feel like?
Passive voice with 'is called'
The passive construction 'is called' is commonly used in academic and educational contexts to introduce or define technical terminology without focusing on who coined the term.
'is called' 수동태 구문은 학술적, 교육적 맥락에서 용어를 만든 사람에 초점을 두지 않고 전문 용어를 소개하거나 정의할 때 자주 사용됩니다.
This reward-based learning process is called positive and negative reinforcement
Called the default mode network were at play.
Not... but (Contrast structure)
This structure sets up a contrast between what is not the case and what actually is. It is used to correct assumptions or redirect the listener's thinking.
'not... but' 구조는 사실이 아닌 것과 실제 사실 사이의 대조를 설정합니다. 가정을 수정하거나 청자의 생각을 전환하는 데 사용됩니다.
the posterior cingulate cortex is activated not necessarily by craving itself, but when we get caught up in it
This isn't to say that, poof, magically we quit smoking, but over time...
Rather than + gerund (Contrasting alternatives)
'Rather than' followed by a gerund is used to present an alternative action, often contrasting a less desirable approach with a preferred one.
'rather than + 동명사'는 대안적 행동을 제시할 때 사용되며, 덜 바람직한 접근법과 더 좋은 접근법을 대조합니다.
Rather than trying to make unpleasant cravings go away as quickly as possible.
rather than getting clobbered by this huge, scary craving that we choke on.
Present perfect for relevance to now
The present perfect tense ('have gone', 'have tried') connects past actions to the present situation, emphasizing that the effects or results are still relevant now.
현재완료 시제('have gone', 'have tried')는 과거의 행동을 현재 상황에 연결하여, 그 효과나 결과가 지금도 여전히 관련이 있음을 강조합니다.
We've gone from learning to survive to literally killing ourselves with these habits.
the majority of them had tried this before and failed,
In other words (Rephrasing signal)
'In other words' is a discourse marker used to restate or clarify a previous idea in simpler or different terms, helping the audience understand a complex concept.
'In other words'는 이전 아이디어를 더 간단하거나 다른 용어로 재진술하거나 명확히 하기 위해 사용되는 담화 표지입니다. 복잡한 개념을 청중이 이해하도록 돕습니다.
In other words, when we get curious, we step out of our old fear-based reactive habit patterns,
Comparative structure with 'twice as... as'
The 'twice as [adjective] as' structure makes a precise comparison, indicating that one thing is double the degree of another. This is common in academic and research contexts.
'twice as [형용사] as' 구조는 정밀한 비교를 하며, 한 가지가 다른 것의 두 배임을 나타냅니다. 학술 및 연구 맥락에서 흔히 사용됩니다.
mindfulness training was twice as good as gold standard therapy at helping people quit smoking.
Instead of + noun/gerund (Substitution)
'Instead of' introduces an alternative to something mentioned, often used to propose a different action or approach. It highlights the contrast between the rejected option and the chosen one.
