04   Grammar Patterns

Key grammar structures used in the video. Each pattern includes real examples.

Conditional Past (If + had + past participle)
Used to describe hypothetical situations in the past that did not actually happen. The 'if' clause uses past perfect, and the main clause uses 'would have + past participle'.
과거에 실제로 일어나지 않은 가정적 상황을 설명할 때 사용합니다. 'if' 절은 과거완료형을 사용하고, 주절은 'would have + 과거분사'를 사용합니다. 제3조건문이라고도 합니다.
If I had never dropped in on that single course in college, the Mac would have never had multiple typefaces.
If I had never dropped out, I would have never dropped in on that calligraphy class.
Gerund as Subject
A gerund (-ing form of a verb) used as the subject of a sentence. This structure treats an action as a noun concept.
동명사(-ing 형태의 동사)를 문장의 주어로 사용하는 구조입니다. 행동을 명사 개념으로 다룹니다.
Remembering that I'll be dead soon is the most important tool I've ever encountered.
Remembering that you are going to die is the best way I know to avoid the trap.
Believing that the dots will connect down the road will give you the confidence.
Parallel Structure (not A but B / A, not B)
Using the same grammatical form for elements that are joined by conjunctions or compared. This creates rhythm and clarity in speech.
접속사로 연결되거나 비교되는 요소에 같은 문법 형태를 사용하는 것입니다. 연설에 리듬감과 명확성을 부여합니다.
The heaviness of being successful was replaced by the lightness of being a beginner again.
You can't connect the dots looking forward. You can only connect them looking backwards.
Imperative for Motivational Commands
The base form of the verb used without a subject to give direct commands, advice, or motivational instructions. Common in speeches to directly address the audience.
주어 없이 동사 원형을 사용하여 직접적인 명령, 조언, 또는 동기부여 지시를 하는 구조입니다. 청중에게 직접 말할 때 연설에서 흔히 사용됩니다.
Stay hungry. Stay foolish.
Don't settle.
Don't lose faith.
Keep looking.
Relative Clause with 'who/that/which'
A clause that provides additional information about a noun, using relative pronouns like who, that, or which. Essential for adding detail and complexity to sentences.
who, that, which 같은 관계대명사를 사용하여 명사에 대한 추가 정보를 제공하는 절입니다. 문장에 세부 사항과 복잡성을 더하는 데 필수적입니다.
We hired someone who I thought was very talented to run the company with me.
An amazing woman who would become my wife.
Don't be trapped by dogma, which is living with the results of other people's thinking.
Phrasal Verbs (drop out, drop in, fall away, dawn on)
Multi-word verbs consisting of a verb plus a preposition or adverb that creates a meaning different from the original verb. Very common in spoken English.
동사에 전치사나 부사가 결합되어 원래 동사와 다른 의미를 만드는 복합 동사입니다. 구어 영어에서 매우 일반적으로 사용됩니다.
I dropped out of Reed College after the first six months.
I could stop taking the required classes and begin dropping in on the ones that looked far more interesting.
Something slowly began to dawn on me.
These things just fall away in the face of death.
Rhetorical Question
A question asked not to get an answer, but to make a point or create emphasis. Used frequently in speeches to engage the audience.
대답을 얻기 위해서가 아니라 논점을 강조하거나 만들기 위해 던지는 질문입니다. 청중의 참여를 유도하기 위해 연설에서 자주 사용됩니다.
So why did I drop out?
How can you get fired from a company you started?
Do you want him?
Passive Voice for Emphasis
Using passive construction (be + past participle) to shift focus from the doer to the action or receiver. Often used to emphasize the experience rather than the agent.
수동태 구문(be + 과거분사)을 사용하여 행위자에서 행동이나 대상으로 초점을 전환합니다. 행위자보다 경험을 강조할 때 자주 사용됩니다.
The heaviness of being successful was replaced by the lightness of being a beginner again.
All of my working class parents' savings were being spent on my college tuition.
I'd been rejected, but I was still in love.
Superlative for Emphasis
Using superlative adjectives (most + adjective or adjective + -est) to express the highest degree, creating powerful emphasis in motivational speech.
최상급 형용사(most + 형용사 또는 형용사 + -est)를 사용하여 최고의 정도를 표현하며, 동기부여 연설에서 강력한 강조를 만듭니다.
It was one of the best decisions I ever made.
Remembering that I'll be dead soon is the most important tool I've ever encountered.
Death is very likely the single best invention of life.
Correlative Conjunctions (as...as, not only...but)
Pairs of conjunctions that work together to join equal grammatical elements. They create balanced, emphatic statements.
같은 문법 요소를 연결하기 위해 함께 사용되는 접속사 쌍입니다. 균형 잡히고 강조적인 문장을 만듭니다.
And that is as true for work as it is for your lovers.
A college that was almost as expensive as Stanford.
Infinitive of Purpose (to + verb)
Using 'to + base verb' to express the purpose or reason for an action. A fundamental structure for explaining motivations.
행동의 목적이나 이유를 표현하기 위해 'to + 동사 원형'을 사용합니다. 동기를 설명하기 위한 기본적인 구조입니다.
I decided to take a calligraphy class to learn how to do this.
I returned Coke bottles for the five-cent deposits to buy food with.
I would walk the seven miles across town every Sunday night to get one good meal a week.
Present Perfect for Life Experience
Using 'have/has + past participle' to describe experiences or actions that happened at an unspecified time in the past and are still relevant now.
과거의 불특정한 시점에 일어났지만 현재까지 관련이 있는 경험이나 행동을 설명할 때 'have/has + 과거분사'를 사용합니다.
If you haven't found it yet, keep looking.
No one has ever escaped it.
I've looked in the mirror every morning and asked myself.

05   Connected Speech & Pronunciation

Natural pronunciation patterns found in the talk. Practice these to sound more natural!

Linking (연음)
dropped out of → 드랍타우러브
/drɑːptˈaʊdəv/
'dropped'의 마지막 't'가 'out'의 모음 'ou'와 연결되고, 'out'은 't'가 'of'의 모음에 연결됩니다.
'드랍-타우-러브'처럼 세 단어를 끊지 않고 자연스럽게 이어서 발음하세요.
connect the dots → 커넥더닷츠
/kəˈnɛktðəˈdɑːts/
'connect'의 마지막 't'가 축약된 'the'로 부드럽게 연결됩니다. 전체 구문이 하나의 단위처럼 흐릅니다.
'커넥-더-닷츠'를 하나의 덩어리로 빠르게 발음하세요.
turned out to be → 턴다우러비
/ˈtɜːrndˈaʊɾəbi/
'turned'의 'd'가 'out'의 모음에 연결되고, 'out'은 플랩 't'를 통해 'to be'로 연결됩니다. 전체 구문이 연속적으로 흐릅니다.
'턴-다우-러-비'처럼 끊지 않고 이어서 발음하세요.
